## Overview

This repository houses the complete design system, copy suite, and engineering specification for an independent, award-winning digital portfolio. It is engineered specifically for a **Growth Marketer & Web Designer** who bridges custom web design, conversion copywriting, and operational AI automation.

The visual direction combines **monumental Apple-grade typography**, a centered portrait featuring an interactive **Liquid ASCII Water Ripple shader**, a **pinned scroll communicating seam** between the Hero and Projects, a **spirit-level fluid nav indicator**, and a **grounded large-type negative footer** with dense ASCII social matrices.

## Photography Brief for Client Asset

Before launching the build, shoot the hero profile portrait according to this brief:
* **Framing:** 90-degree side profile or 3/4 side profile looking slightly forward, centered.
* **Lighting:** High-contrast rim/key lighting from the side or slightly behind. Deep shadows across the far side of the face with sharp highlight along the profile silhouette.
* **Wardrobe:** Solid dark crewneck, high-collar jacket, or dark hood. Zero logos or bright patterns.
* **Backdrop:** Solid dark charcoal or matte black backdrop. The background will feather seamlessly into `#080A0F`.
* **Destination Path:** Save the image to `public/assets/portrait.png`.
